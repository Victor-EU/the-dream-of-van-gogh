Wheatfield with a ReaperThe order in which the strokes arrive is reconstructed, not recorded. Where two marks overlap the paint says which was laid second, and 8,172 of those readings are strong enough to use; withhold a fifth of them and the reconstruction gets 52.8% of the withheld ones right against 46.0% for the painter's habits alone. So the order is partly evidence and mostly habit, and it correlates 0.89 with the habits it started from. The timing is invention throughout.
